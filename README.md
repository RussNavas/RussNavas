# Howdy! My name is Russell Navas,

I got into computer science and software engineering during my first degree in Biological Chemistry at *The University of Texas at Arlington*. I took a course in Bioinformatics where I learned about Linux, the command line, Python, data structures and algorithms. This was also during the height of the pandemic and I was amazed that I could help out a PhD student with their bioinformatics project with just a Macbook and internet access. It was really an empowering moment coming from Chemistry where so much of my hands on experience required so much physical material and expensive instrumentation, not to mention the cost; but here I was doing science and contributing miles away form the university and I did not need to have a giant lab to play. I was really inspired by that moment which was my last year in undergrad. It was a hard decision but I decided I wanted to finish my degree and enroll for a second degree in CS at *Oregon State University*, to fund this degree I took a job teaching high school and dual credit chemistry full time and through great effort and self discipline I got it done! I left the classroom, relocated, and began a new adventure! Life is short and I want to make the most of it and keep learning new things and taking on tough challenges.

## My coding interest:
I like to write backend systems in Python and Go, I am also having fun with TypeScript and Sveltekit on the frontend. I have recently finished superttt.app which is a full stack web app that lets you play a game of super tic tac toe over the network and chat in real time. The game features leaderboards and matchmaking, please feel free to check out my repo and site!
